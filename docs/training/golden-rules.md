# Guest ordering — Golden rules

For every staff member. Read it. Keep it.

1. Guests ask. Staff confirm. Nothing happens until you accept it.

2. The screen is the order. Not WhatsApp. Not a promise at the table.

3. "I've paid" is only a claim. Check the money first. Then mark paid.

4. Accept a table, own its money. A walk-out is your shortfall.

5. Never give guests your personal number. They use Call waiter.

6. Start your shift on time. Guests can see when the bar is late.

7. Guests pay the price they saw. Even if the price changes later.

8. Turn sounds on every morning. Tap the sound bar on every screen.

9. Change an order only in the system. Always give a reason.

10. Refused drinks go back to the bar at once.
