# Cashier — What changes with guest QR orders

Text in bold is copied exactly from the screen.

## 1. Your job in guest ordering
You confirm the waiters' money exactly as before.

## 2. Start of shift
- Nothing new. Start as you always do.

## 3. When this happens → do this

#### A waiter was paid with cash and transfer together
- You will see two separate payment lines.
- Example: ₦8,000 transfer and ₦10,000 cash.
- Confirm each one in its own channel, as normal.

#### A transfer line shows a payer name
1. Open **Transfer Queue**.
2. Look at the **Payer Ref** column.
3. The name often comes from the guest's "I've paid" message.
4. Match it with the bank alert.
5. Tap **Verify**. Or **Flag** if it is wrong.

#### The blind confirmation
- Nothing has changed.
- You still count without seeing the waiter's figures.

## 4. Never do this
- Never verify a transfer you cannot see in the bank.
- Never trust a payer name on its own.
- Never merge two payment lines into one.

## 5. If something goes wrong

| Problem | What to do / who to call |
|---|---|
| Name does not match the bank alert | Tap **Flag**. Tell the manager. |
| Transfer missing from the bank | Tap **Flag**. The waiter must explain. |
