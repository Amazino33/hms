# Receptionist — Room orders

Text in bold is copied exactly from the screen.

## 1. Your job in guest ordering
You approve room orders, send them up, and handle room payments.

## 2. Start of shift
1. Keep the venue WhatsApp phone at the desk, charged.
2. Open **Room Orders** in the admin menu.
3. Tap **Tap to enable order sounds**.
4. Leave the page open. It refreshes by itself.

[Photo: Room Orders page with three columns]

## 3. When this happens → do this

#### A new order appears under New orders
1. Look at the Ref. Example: R7-0423.
2. Find the WhatsApp message with the same Ref.
3. Check the items match the screen.
4. Confirm with the guest on WhatsApp.
5. Tap **Approve**.
6. Food goes to the kitchen. Drinks go to the bar.

#### The card says No WhatsApp — call the room
1. Phone the room.
2. Read the order back to the guest.
3. Then tap **Approve**.

#### The card says New phone
- This phone has not ordered for this room before.
- Check it is really the guest. Call the room if unsure.

#### Saving the guest's number
1. In the approve box, fill **Guest WhatsApp number (from chat)**.
2. Tick **Agreed to receive specials** ONLY if they said yes.
3. Tap **Approve**.

#### The guest wants fewer items
1. Tap **−** to give fewer. Or **✕** to remove.
2. Pick a reason. Tap **Confirm**.
- You cannot add items.
- To add, the guest orders again from the phone.

#### You cannot serve the order at all
1. Tap **Reject**.
2. Type a reason. The guest sees it.
3. Tap **Reject** again.

#### An order appears under To deliver
1. Give the tray to a porter.
2. Tap **Send with…**.
3. Tap the porter's name. Or **Reception** if you carry it.
- The guest sees "On the way with Musa".

#### The porter comes back (Out for delivery)
- The guest took it: tap **Delivered**.
- The guest refused it: tap **Refused**.
1. Type why. Example: "Wrong drink".
2. Tap **Record refusal**.
- Nothing comes off the bill yet. A manager decides.

#### A purple card says a room guest paid
- This is only what the guest SAYS.
1. Check the transfer arrived in the account.
2. Money arrived: tap **Open folio payment**.
3. Check the amount and name. Tap **Save payment**.
4. No money: tap **Not received**.

#### A blue card appears (Call reception)
1. Read it. Example: "Room 7 · Cutlery".
2. Tap **On it**.
3. Send someone to the room.

## 4. Never do this
- Never change an order because of a chat message.
- Change it here in the system, with a reason.
- Never approve before the Ref matches.
- Never tick specials unless the guest said yes.
- Never save a payment before the money arrives.
- Never give guests your personal number.

## 5. If something goes wrong

| Problem | What to do / who to call |
|---|---|
| **Approve** refuses: guest checked out | Tap **Reject**. Tell the manager. |
| WhatsApp message has no matching card | Ask the guest to order on the menu again. |
| Chat asks for extra items | Ask the guest to order them from the phone. |
| Transfer not in the account | Tap **Not received**. Tell the manager. |
| No sound | Tap **Tap to enable order sounds**. |
