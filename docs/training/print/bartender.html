Bartender — Guest QR orders

Text in bold is copied exactly from the screen.

1. Your job in guest ordering

You get guest drinks ready from the bar screen.

2. Start of shift

  1. Start your shift BEFORE guests arrive.
  2. Use Go to My Handover Count to start it.
  3. Open the bar screen. Find Pending Orders.
  4. Tap Tap to enable order sounds.
  5. Check that Sound off has gone away.

3. When this happens → do this

A guest card appears (red GUEST strip)

  1. Pour the drinks on the card.
  2. Read the extras in red. Example: "Cold".
  3. Tap MARK READY. It goes on the guest's bill.

A drink is short or finished

  1. Tap − to give fewer. Or ✕ to remove it.
  2. Pick a reason: Out of stock, Wrong item or Other.
  3. For Other, type what happened.
  4. Tap Confirm.
  • The guest sees your reason on their phone.

A red banner says guests are waiting

  • It says "… start your shift to mark them ready."
  • Start your shift now. Guests can see you are late.

Asked: Who is marking this ready?

  • Two shifts are open. Tap your own name.
  • Then end the old shift. The amber banner will go.

A card shows GUEST · Room

  • A hotel room order. Tap MARK READY as normal.
  • Stock and the room charge happen at that moment.
  • Reception sends it up to the room.

Returned from rooms

  1. Wait until you can count the bottles.
  2. Tap Returned ✓.
  • A manager then decides the refusal.

Your shift ends

  • Waiting drinks stay for the next bartender.

4. Never do this

  • Never tap MARK READY before the drinks are poured.
  • Never tap Returned ✓ before the bottles are back.
  • Never remove a drink without a true reason.
  • Never give guests your personal phone number.

5. If something goes wrong

ProblemWhat to do / who to call
