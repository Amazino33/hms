1. Your job in guest ordering

You cook guest orders and keep the sold-out list correct.

2. Start of shift

  1. Start your chef shift on time.
  2. Open the kitchen screen.
  3. Tap Sold out.
  4. Tap Sign in and enter your PIN.
  5. Tap Reset all to available.
  6. Mark anything you really do not have.
  7. Tap Done.
Photo: kitchen screen with the "Sold out?" panel open

3. When this happens → do this

A guest order arrives

  • It looks like any other ticket.
  • Read the extras and notes in bold. Example: "Extra pepper".
  • Cook it. Mark it ready as normal.
  • Room food also goes out when you mark it ready.

You run out of a dish

  1. Tap Sold out.
  2. Find the dish. Example: Egusi Soup.
  3. Tap its green Available button.
  4. It turns red: Sold out.
  5. Tap Done.
  • Guests stop seeing it within about a minute and a half.

The dish is back

  1. Tap Sold out.
  2. Tap the red Sold out button on that dish.
  3. It turns green: Available.

4. Never do this

  • Never let a waiter use the sold-out panel.
  • A waiter's PIN is refused there anyway.
  • Never mark a dish sold out "just in case".
  • Never skip Reset all to available in the morning.

5. If something goes wrong
