1. Your job in guest ordering

You carry guest room orders up, and tell reception what happened.

2. Start of shift

  1. Tell reception you are on duty.
  2. Stay near the desk when you are free.
  • Reception records guest deliveries on the Room Orders page.
  • You do not tap anything for guest QR orders.
  • Guest orders do not show on Porter Deliveries.

3. When this happens → do this

Reception hands you a tray

  1. Check the room number and the items.
  2. Reception taps your name. The guest sees "On the way with Musa".
  3. Go straight to the room.

The guest takes the order

  1. Come back to the desk.
  2. Tell reception: "Delivered".
  3. Reception taps Delivered.

The guest refuses the order

  1. Ask the guest why. Remember the reason.
  2. Bring everything back. Do not leave it in the room.
  3. Tell reception the reason. They tap Refused.
  4. Take refused drinks to the bar IMMEDIATELY.
  5. Unopened bottles go back to the bartender.
  6. Take refused food back to the kitchen.
Photo: porter handing refused bottles to the bartender

4. Never do this

  • Never leave a refused order in the room.
  • Never keep refused drinks. Return them at once.
  • Never take money from a room guest for an order.
  • Never give guests your personal phone number.

5. If something goes wrong

ProblemWhat to do / who to call
Nobody opens the doorCome back. Tell reception.
Wrong room on the trayStop. Go back to reception.
Guest wants to pay youSay "It goes on your room bill".
Guest wants more itemsAsk them to order from their phone.
