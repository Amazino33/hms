Waiter — Guest QR orders

Text in bold is copied exactly from the screen.

1. Your job in guest ordering

Guests order from their phones. You confirm, serve and collect the money.

2. Start of shift

  1. Start your shift on time.
  2. Look at the Guest orders box on the kiosk.
  3. Tap Tap to enable order sounds.
  4. Check that Sound off has gone away.
  5. Do this every morning, on every kiosk.
Photo: waiter kiosk with the "Guest orders" box and the sound bar

3. When this happens → do this

A new guest order card appears (it beeps)

  1. Read the table, the items and the notes in yellow.
  2. Tap Accept.
  3. Enter your PIN.
  4. Food goes to the kitchen. Drinks go to the bar.
  5. This table is now yours. Its money is yours too.

The screen asks "Same guests?"

  • It means the table already has an unpaid bill.
  • Tap Yes, same guests if it is the same group.
  • Their old bill joins the new one.
  • Tap No, different if these are new guests.
  • The old bill stays separate. Settle it on its own.
  • Not sure? Ask the guests before you tap.

A card is grey

  • It belongs to another waiter. Example: "Emeka's table".
  • Leave it for them.

A red card says Returned from bar

  • That table's waiter has gone off shift.
  • Tap Take this table, then your PIN.
  • The table and its money are now yours.

A blue card appears (Call waiter)

  1. Read it. Example: "Table 5 · Ice".
  2. Tap On my way. No PIN is needed.
  3. Go to the table now.

A purple card says a guest paid

  • Example: "Table 5 · Ada says they paid ₦8,000 by transfer".
  • This is only what the guest SAYS.
  • Check the money really arrived before you mark paid.

Taking payment (cash and transfer together)

  1. Open the table on the kiosk.
  2. Confirm every ready order with Confirm Served.
  3. Tap Split by method (e.g. part cash, part transfer).
  4. Guest claims are filled in for you. Check them.
  5. Change any amount if needed.
  6. Tap Confirm · settle when Remaining shows ₦0.
  • One method only? Tap Cash, POS or Transfer.
  • An amber warning means a claim has no transfer line. Fix it, or tap Continue.
  • The full payment screen will not work for guest tables.
  • You will see "Guest QR table — use Mark Paid."

Guests want another table

  1. Find the table under Guest tables.
  2. Tap Move table.
  3. Pick a free table.
  4. Enter your PIN.
  5. Tell the guests to scan the new table's code.

Guests are leaving

  1. Make sure everything is paid.
  2. Tap Close table, then your PIN.
  3. If it refuses, read the list. Settle those first.
  • Only close when the guests have really left.

You want to end your shift

  • You cannot end it while drinks wait at the bar.
  1. Tap End Shift. The screen shows Hand over your guest tables first.
  2. Pick the table.
  3. Choose a waiter in Hand to….
  4. They type their own PIN.
  5. Tap Hand over.

4. Never do this

  • Never give a guest your personal phone number.
  • Never mark paid before checking a transfer arrived.
  • Never accept a table you will not serve.
  • Never close a table while guests are still sitting.
  • Never use another waiter's PIN.
