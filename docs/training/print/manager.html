Manager / Supervisor — Guest QR orders

Text in bold is copied exactly from the screen.

1. Your job in guest ordering

You watch the alerts, decide refusals and look after the QR codes.

2. Start of shift

  1. Check the bell in the admin panel for alerts.
  2. Walk past every kiosk and bar screen.
  3. Make sure Sound off is not showing anywhere.
  4. Check the kitchen tapped Reset all to available.

3. When this happens → do this

Alert: … no bartender shift started

  • Guests waited 5 minutes. Start a bartender shift now.

Alert: … end the old one

  • Two bartender shifts are open. End the old one.

Alert: Room delivery refused

  1. Open Delivery Refusals.
  2. Drinks show Waiting for the bar until the bottles return.
  3. When it shows Waiting for a manager, decide.
  4. Tap Approve — reverse the charge if the refusal is fair.
  5. Tap Reject — charge stands if the guest must pay.
  6. Add a short note. Confirm.
  • Approved: drinks go back to stock; food becomes waste.

You need the guest marketing list

  1. Open Guest Contacts.
  2. Tap Export opted-in (CSV).
  • Only guests who agreed are in the file.
  • A guest agrees later? Tap Agreed to specials on their row.

A QR code is lost or misused

  1. Open QR Codes.
  2. Find the table or room.
  3. Tap Regenerate.
  4. Type the reason. Example: "Card stolen".
  5. Tap Replace QR code. Print and place the new code.
  • The old code now shows "no longer valid".

Every night (first two weeks)

  1. Check Delivery Refusals for anything waiting.
  2. Ask reception about claims marked Not received.
  3. Late bar starts: no screen yet. Ask the developer.

4. Never do this

  • Never approve a refusal before the bar confirms the bottles.
  • Never export contacts who did not agree.
  • Never regenerate a code without printing the new one.

5. If something goes wrong

ProblemWhat to do / who to call
Regenerate done by mistakePrint the new code. The old one cannot return.
Refusal shows Waiting for the bar for longAsk the bartender to check the bottles.
Supervisor cannot decide a refusalOnly a manager can (see discrepancies.md).
